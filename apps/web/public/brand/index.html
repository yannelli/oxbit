Spacing and color

Leave at least one square bit of space around the mark. Use mint on dark backgrounds and teal on light backgrounds. Keep the O and bit together. The favicon has wider gaps for small sizes.

Typography and files

The wordmark uses Instrument Sans Bold with adjusted spacing. Logo and banner lettering is outlined in the SVGs. The variants collection includes opaque and transparent PNG logos. The kit includes SVG, PNG, ICO, and browser icon files.
